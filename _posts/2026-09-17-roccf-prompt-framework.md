# Use the ROCCF Framework to Structure Every AI Prompt

Use the **ROCCF** framework (**Role, Objective, Constraints, Context, Format**) to structure every AI prompt for reliable, high-quality results.

## The ROCCF Prompt Structure

### R – Role

Tell the AI who it is (e.g., *"Act as an expert copywriter"* or *"You are a senior financial analyst"*).

### O – Objective

Explain the exact task or problem you want solved and why you need it.

### C – Constraints

Set clear boundaries, rules, or limits (e.g., *"Keep it under 300 words"* or *"Do not use jargon"*).

### C – Context

Provide background facts, the target audience, or situational details.

### F – Format

Specify how the final output should look (e.g., *"A bulleted list"*, *"A table"*, or *"Markdown layout"*).

## Copy-and-Paste Template

**Role:** Act as a [insert expert role].

**Objective:** [Explain what you want the AI to do and the goal behind it].

**Constraints:** [List rules, limits, or things to avoid].

**Context:** [Provide background info, audience, or source details].

**Format:** [Specify how the final answer should be presented].

## Example Usage

Would you like me to **adapt this template for a specific task** you have in mind right now (such as writing an email, coding, or summarizing notes)?
